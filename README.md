# Sonic 3 A.I.R. — Project Metal Hedge

A fan-made **Sonic 3 A.I.R. story mod** featuring Sonic and Eggman's new robotic creation: **Metal Hedge**, a green robotic hedgehog built to challenge Sonic.

## Story

Dr. Eggman has created Metal Hedge, a machine designed to imitate Sonic's speed and stop him. Eggman sends his new creation to Angel Island, where Sonic begins to uncover the secret project.

At first, Metal Hedge watches Sonic from a distance before escaping. Sonic follows the trail toward a later confrontation. The story and encounters will grow as development continues.

> **Important:** Sonic is the main protagonist and remains playable. Hedge is not a character in this story. Metal Hedge is the robotic antagonist created by Dr. Eggman.

## Current status

## Requirements and compatibility

- **Game:** Sonic 3 A.I.R.
- **Game data:** A legally obtained, compatible Sonic 3 & Knuckles ROM is required by the game.
